Frequently Asked Questions

Judge GPT TV is a fictional steampunk courtroom satire exploring emerging technology and the moral, social, and legal questions it can create.

The website connects issues raised in the episodes with relevant real-world policies, proposed legislation, and potential policy gaps. Learn more on our About page .

No. Judge GPT TV is fictional entertainment. Its characters, courtroom proceedings, disputes, and fictional legal systems should not be interpreted as actual legal proceedings or legal advice.

However, the technological and social issues explored by the episodes may have real-world parallels. The website is designed to help viewers explore those connections.

Judge GPT TV is designed to remain politically neutral.

Its purpose is not to tell viewers what to believe or which position to support. Instead, it presents relevant policy information and authoritative sources so viewers can consider the issue and form their own views.

Where civic-action tools are provided, viewers choose their own position.

Each episode has its own page , beginning with the fictional case and the Real-World Issue(s) it raises.

From there, viewers can explore relevant policy information—including existing policies, proposed policies, and potential policy gaps—and use Take Action (on episode pages) if they choose to communicate their own views.

Each episode is reviewed to identify the specific real-world issues raised by its fictional scenario.

Research is then conducted to identify relevant existing policies, proposed policies, and potential policy gaps. Episode pages present this information in plain language and provide links to authoritative sources wherever available.

A Potential Policy Gap means that Judge GPT TV's research did not identify a policy that fully protects constituents from, or otherwise fully addresses, the specific issue being examined.

It does not mean that no relevant law, regulation, policy, legal remedy, or protection exists.

Policy research can be complex, and laws change. Potential Policy Gaps are therefore presented cautiously and may be revised as additional information or new policies are identified.

Judge GPT TV provides policy information for educational, civic-information, and entertainment-related purposes. It does not provide legal advice.

Viewers should review the linked authoritative sources themselves and seek qualified professional advice when appropriate.

Wherever practical, Judge GPT TV links directly to authoritative sources, such as government legislation, regulations, executive actions, official policy documents, and other primary sources.

Viewers are encouraged to review those original sources rather than relying solely on Judge GPT TV's summaries.

Take Action provides neutral tools to help viewers communicate their own views about an issue or policy.

Depending on the issue, viewers may choose to Support, Oppose, Request Amendment, or Request Action on a Potential Policy Gap.

Judge GPT TV provides standard boilerplate text appropriate to the selected action, together with free-form space where viewers can provide their own opinions and explain why the issue matters to them. Viewer-written text is used exactly as entered and is not rewritten by AI.

Judge GPT TV does not choose a position for the viewer.

Bills can be amended, enacted, rejected, withdrawn, or otherwise change status. Regulations and executive actions can also change, and new policies may address an issue previously identified as a Potential Policy Gap.

Judge GPT TV may therefore update Episode pages and the Policy Archive as material developments occur.

Visitors are encouraged to return to the website and check Current Status / Outcomes to see what happened after a policy was featured or acted upon.

The Policy Archive is the comprehensive archive of policies covered by Judge GPT TV, organized by episode.

Episode-specific policies can also be found directly within the corresponding Episode page. The Archive provides another way to review covered policies, their status and outcomes, and the episodes associated with them.

“Comprehensive” refers to Judge GPT TV's approved policy coverage. The Policy Archive is not intended to be a comprehensive database of all laws or public policy.
