LEGAL

Legal & Privacy

Fictional Content

Judge GPT TV is fictional entertainment.

Judge GPT TV is a fictional steampunk courtroom satire. Its cases, characters, testimony, courts, rulings, laws, institutions, technologies, and events are fictional unless expressly identified as real-world information.

Fictional courtroom proceedings and rulings should not be interpreted as statements of actual law, actual legal proceedings, or the legal rights or obligations of any person.

Real-world policy information presented alongside an episode is clearly distinguished from the fictional case.

Privacy

Judge GPT TV is designed with your privacy in mind.

You do not need to create an account, join a mailing list, or provide personal information to use the site's civic-information or Take Action features.

Judge GPT TV does not maintain a database recording which covered policies individual viewers support or oppose, or connecting individual viewers with their political communications.

Text entered by viewers into the Take Action feature is not permanently stored and is erased when the viewer leaves that feature. Judge GPT TV does not automatically send those communications.

The Contact form is designed for anonymous submissions and does not require a name or email address. Visitors may voluntarily include contact information within their message if they wish. Where personal information is voluntarily provided, Judge GPT TV retains only the minimum information reasonably necessary to process the communication.

Policy Information & AI

Judge GPT TV uses artificial intelligence to assist with research, analysis, writing, translation, website operation, and other functions.

Real-world policy summaries and other AI-assisted information may contain errors or omit important details. Judge GPT TV therefore provides links to authoritative sources wherever practical, and viewers are encouraged to review those sources directly.

Policy information may also change after publication. Judge GPT TV may update its summaries, classifications, status information, outcomes, and Potential Policy Gaps as new information becomes available.

Potential Policy Gaps

A Potential Policy Gap means Judge GPT TV's research did not identify a policy that fully protects constituents from, or otherwise fully addresses, the specific issue examined.

It does not mean that no relevant law, regulation, policy, legal remedy, or protection exists.

No Legal Advice

Information provided by Judge GPT TV is for informational, educational, civic, and entertainment-related purposes and does not constitute legal advice.

Use of this website does not create an attorney-client relationship. Anyone requiring advice regarding their individual legal rights, obligations, or circumstances should consult an appropriately qualified professional.

Civic Participation

Judge GPT TV does not tell viewers which political position to take. Viewers decide for themselves whether to Support, Oppose, Request Amendment, Request Action on a Potential Policy Gap, or take no action.

Take Action templates are provided as tools for viewers to express their own views. Judge GPT TV does not automatically send communications to representatives or public officials.

Third-Party Resources

Judge GPT TV may provide links to government websites, authoritative policy sources, representative or government-contact services, video platforms, and other third-party resources.

These services operate independently and are subject to their own terms and privacy practices. Providing a link does not necessarily constitute endorsement of the third party or its views.
