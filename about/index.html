About Judge GPT TV

The case is fictional. The issue may not be.

Judge GPT TV is a fictional steampunk courtroom satire that explores scenarios identifying moral and social gaps between technological innovation and legislation.

Through fictional cases involving emerging technologies and their consequences, Judge GPT TV uses entertainment as a starting point for examining real-world questions about law and public policy.

The Judge GPT TV website extends those stories beyond the courtroom. It connects issues raised in each episode with relevant real-world policies, proposed legislation, and potential policy gaps. Viewers can explore the underlying issues, review authoritative sources, follow policy developments, and—if they choose—use neutral tools to communicate their own views to the appropriate public officials.

Judge GPT TV does not tell viewers what position to take. Its purpose is to help people understand the issue, consider the available information, and decide for themselves.

Mission

To use fictional satire as a gateway to civic understanding by connecting emerging-technology scenarios with verified real-world policy and giving viewers neutral tools to form and communicate their own views.

Vision

A globally adaptable civic-entertainment platform where audiences are informed and empowered in shaping how law and public policy respond to technological change.
